<u>By the end of 2020</u>, I had an accident that lead to a fracture in my cervical bones. The toughest part was that this happened two weeks before my 9th-semester finals, meaning that I had to postpone my exams for the following year to have an urgent surgery, which meant I would postpone my graduation and my future plans as well. I decided not to give up on my aspiration or waste the effort of the past years. I studied my courses till the last minute before my surgery and continued studying <u>until</u> the moment I left the ICU. At that moment, I didn't care about the outcome as much as I cared about doing all I can. As a result, I graduated <u>a valedictorian</u> in 2021 with an overall grade of excellent with the highest honors. <u>I decided</u> to keep doing the thing I love, research. I applied to the XXX Master's fellowship, and I was accepted <u>into</u> the XXX department, to start a new chapter of my academic life.
